Local Intel Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Local Intel is a personal Android app that processes the user's own email on their phone. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Gmail messages. With the user's permission (Google sign-in), the app connects to the user's Gmail inbox and reads newly arriving messages. It opens the mailbox read-only: it does not send, delete, modify, or mark messages as read.
  • Google account email address, used only to connect to that mailbox.

How the data is used

Messages are analyzed on the phone by an on-device language model to recognize actionable items, such as a pickup order. When the user confirms an action, the app passes the minimum needed to another app on the phone. For example, it hands a pickup address to the Tesla app to set the car's navigation destination.

Storage and sharing

  • Email content, analysis results and activity logs are stored only on the phone, in the app's private storage.
  • The app has no servers. No email content or personal data is sent to the developer or to any third party, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.
  • The only data that leaves the phone is what the user explicitly confirms, such as a destination address passed to the Tesla app.

Google user data

Local Intel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored. Gmail access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
