Marian's Local Intel

A personal, on-device email assistant for Android.

What the app does

Marian's Local Intel is a personal Android app that reads incoming email on the user's phone and helps act on it. Its first feature handles pickup orders: when an order confirmation (for example from a restaurant) arrives, the app recognizes the pickup address and ready time and shows a notification. One tap on Send to car passes the pickup address to the Tesla app, which sets the car's navigation destination.

How it uses Google data

  • The user signs in with Google and grants access to their Gmail (https://mail.google.com/, needed to connect to Gmail over IMAP).
  • The app opens the inbox read-only and reads only newly arriving messages. It never sends, deletes, modifies or marks messages as read.
  • Messages are analyzed entirely on the phone by an on-device language model that extracts text fields such as a merchant name, address and pickup time. Email content is never uploaded to any server; the app has no servers.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create, generate or edit images, audio or video of any kind, and does not use Google APIs or Google user data for AI-generated imagery, including AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII).
  • It does not use Google user data to train AI models, for advertising, or for any purpose other than the features described above.
  • It does not share Google user data with third parties.

Marian's Local Intel is a personal project and is not publicly distributed. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
