Local Intel

A personal, on-device assistant for Android.

Local Intel watches the user's own email on their phone, understands it with a language model that runs entirely on the device, and helps act on it. For example, when a pickup order confirmation arrives, it offers to send the pickup address to the user's car navigation.

All processing happens on the phone. Local Intel has no servers and does not send email content anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Local Intel is a personal project and is not publicly distributed.
